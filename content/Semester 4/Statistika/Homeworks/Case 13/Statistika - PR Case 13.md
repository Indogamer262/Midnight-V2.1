Sample Variance ($s^2$) = 
$$
s^2 = \frac{\sum (\bar{x}-x\tiny{i})^2}{n-1}
$$
$$
s = \sqrt{s^2}
$$
$$
\sqrt{n} = 2Z_{\frac{\alpha}{2}} {\times \frac{\sigma}{W}}
$$
