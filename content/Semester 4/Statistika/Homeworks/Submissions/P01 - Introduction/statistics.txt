 Data Classification
-------------------------------------------------------------
 Definitions:
 * Qualitative data (attribute, labels, non-numerical entries)
 * Quantitative data (numbers that are counts or measurement)

 Levels of measurement
 ---------------------
 [attachment: SummaryOfLevelsOfMeasurement.png]
  * Nominal Level
      --> String/Non-Number
  * Ordinal Level: Bukan angka yang dikonversi menjadi angka
      --> Rating 1-5 (Sangat Buruk -- Sangat Baik)
          data kualitatif yang dapat dikuantifikasikan
          (likert scale) _____________________________________
  * Interval Level      | --> basically "Range" !!            |
  * Ratio Level         | Range/Rasio                         |
                        | Many mathematical tools (algorithm) |
                        | to be used                          |
                        |_____________________________________|
