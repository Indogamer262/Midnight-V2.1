> [!note]- P4 - Conditional Probability
> ![[P4-IMG_20260322_071733.jpg]]
> # Catatan Tambahan
> Menentukan Union dari P(A) dan P(B)
> - Dependent Relationship
> 	$$
> 	P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B) - (P(A) \times P(B|A))
> 	$$
> 	dan
> 	$$
> 	P(A \cap B) = P(A) \times P(B|A)
> 	$$
> 	> [!info] dengan
> 	> $$
> 	> P(B|A) = \frac{P(B \cap A)}{P(A)}
> 	> $$
> - Independent Relationship
> 	Ciri-cirinya adalah P(A|B) = P(A)​
> 	$$
> 	P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B) - (P(A) \times P(B))
> 	$$
> 	dan
> 	$$
> 	P(A \cap B) = P(A) \times P(B)
> 	$$
